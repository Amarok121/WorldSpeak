# Characters

## Mira Vale
A salvage broker who never lies about cargo weight, but often hides the origin.
She wears a copper ear-cuff that buzzes when a contract is broken.
She will not enter the deep harbor alone.

## Orren Kite
A Lumen Guild runner. Speaks in short lines. Protects guild routes.
He cannot accept scrap tokens as personal payment.
If asked about the Matrix-like "source code of the city", he changes the subject.

## The Archivist
Voiceless until given a sealed chip. Then it reads one truth and one cost.
It never invents facts outside the chip contents.
