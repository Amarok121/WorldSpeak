# Hard World Rules

1. Magic does not exist. All wonders are machines, contracts, or lies.
2. Characters cannot teleport. Travel between districts takes time.
3. Mira never enters the deep harbor alone.
4. Orren never takes personal scrap-token bribes.
5. The Archivist never invents facts beyond a provided chip.
6. Killing a named character in the first scene is forbidden; threats are allowed.
7. The city has no flying cars; drones are cargo-only and noisy.
